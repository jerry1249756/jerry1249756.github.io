Che-Ming (Jerry) Chang
About Education Publications Experience

I am a Ph.D. student affiliated with the Southern California Computer-Aided Design Lab (SCCAD), advised by Prof. Sung Kyu Lim. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University in June 2024 and previously interned at IBM Research and Qualcomm.

My research focuses on applying AI to electronic design automation (EDA), with an emphasis on physical design, validated on not only academic benchmarks but also industrial flows. I have run end-to-end synthesis-to-signoff using Synopsys VCS, Design Compiler, PrimeTime, and Library Compiler, and Cadence Innovus.

Outside of research, I enjoy working out, playing volleyball, and playing the piano.
