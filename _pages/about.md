<p style="text-align: justify;">
I am a Ph.D. student affiliated with <a href="https://sites.usc.edu/sccad/" style="color: #4287f5;">Southern California Computer-Aided Design Lab (SCCAD)</a>, advised by Prof. Sung Kyu Lim. I received my B.S. in Electrical Engineering from National Taiwan University in June 2024 and previously interned at IBM Research and Qualcomm.

<p style="text-align: justify;">
My research focuses on <strong>applying AI to electronic design automation (EDA)</strong>, with an emphasis on physical design, validated on not only academic benchmarks but on industrial flows. I've run end-to-end synthesis-to-signoff using <strong>Synopsys VCS, Design Compiler, PrimeTime, and Library Compiler</strong>, and <strong>Cadence Innovus</strong>.

Outside of research, I enjoy working out, playing volleyball, and playing the piano.
